# Always Active Guidelines: Superpowers, UI/UX Pro Max & Perplexity Super-Skills

Always follow the **Superpowers** software development methodology, **UI/UX Pro Max** design intelligence, **Humanizer** natural writing standards, and **Perplexity Super-Skills** domain expertise.

Before starting any task, responding with plans, or exploring:
1. Check available skills under `.agents/skills/`.
2. Always invoke relevant skills before action or answering:
   - **brainstorming**: For new features, ideas, or architectural designs.
   - **systematic-debugging**: For investigating root causes and fixing bugs.
   - **test-driven-development**: For implementing changes through red/green/refactor loops.
   - **writing-plans** & **executing-plans**: For multi-step implementations.
   - **verification-before-completion**: For comprehensive verification before declaring a task done.
   - **subagent-driven-development**: For executing independent steps cleanly.
   - **ui-ux-pro-max**: For all UI/UX design, landing pages, mobile/web interfaces, color palettes, accessibility, charts, and design systems.
   - **humanizer**: For natural, human-grade writing and eliminating robotic AI writing artifacts.
   - **dev-engineering** & **ai-agent**: For full-stack engineering, architecture, testing, CI/CD, and agent pipelines.
   - **token-efficient**: For high-signal, concise, and focused communication.
3. Announce: `"Using [skill] to [purpose]"` and follow the skill's directives strictly.
